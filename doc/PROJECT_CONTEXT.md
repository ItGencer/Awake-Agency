# Project Context

## Purpose

Awake is a modern, premium one-page website for a creative digital agency. The page is intended to convert startup and business leads by presenting strategy, design, development, work, team, testimonials, pricing, FAQ, awards, and a final CTA in a polished editorial layout.

## Audience

- Startup founders comparing creative partners.
- Small and mid-sized businesses looking for brand, web, or digital experience work.
- Prospective clients evaluating pricing, team credibility, and past project style.
